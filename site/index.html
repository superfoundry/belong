What makes Belong different

Matched both ways

You only meet people who are looking for someone like you, and who are what you're looking for.

Real people, one account each

Everyone passes an ID check before they can list anything, so there are no fake accounts.

No cold messages

Nobody can message you before you both say yes, and nobody is ever told who said no.

Seen only by your matches

There are no profiles to scroll and nobody can look you up. Your listing only reaches the people it fits.

Some things are just for matching

Your AI drafts your listing, and you choose what's shown. Hidden details still help find the right people, and are never shown to anyone you match with or to any app.

Belong runs the matching, so it can see what it holds. It keeps as little as it can.
