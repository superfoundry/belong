How it works

  1. 1

    Say what you're looking for

    In your own words, to the AI you already use or in the Belong app. Your AI turns it into a listing.

  2. 2

    Belong finds who's looking for you

    It compares your listing with everyone else's. There are no profiles to scroll, and nobody browses anyone.

  3. 3

    Meet when you both say yes

    Until you both say yes, nobody can reach you. Nobody is ever told who said no.

Some things are just for matching

Your AI drafts your listing, and you choose what's shown. Hidden details still help find the right people, and are never shown to anyone you match with or to any app.

Belong runs the matching, so it can see what it holds. It keeps as little as it can.

Use Belong your way

In the Belong app, with the AI you already use, or in something you build.
